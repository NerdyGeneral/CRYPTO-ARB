A suspect with no barrier found means the rule may have been too cautious. A traded route with a barrier means it wasn't cautious enough: with money already on both exchanges one trade still goes through, but closed transfers or a different token stop you rebalancing, so the gap can't be repeated.

Paper only. Buy a coin on the cheapest exchange and short the same amount of its US perpetual future on Coinbase (Coinbase Financial Markets, CFTC-regulated). Price moves cancel out; while the future trades above spot, the short collects the hourly funding that longs pay.

Open positions

Coins held on the spot exchange, short futures on Coinbase. "Net if closed now" sells the coins at the bid and buys the short back at the ask, fees included. Margin shows how far the futures price can rise before the short's collateral falls below the overnight margin; positions close automatically before 10%.

Perpetual futures

Funding rates as yearly rates. "Last hour" is the latest settlement, "24h avg" the average of those recorded here; the lower of the two is what an entry expects.
